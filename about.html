About

Kiki Ogino paints quiet interiors — attics, salons, bedrooms, kitchens — places where light settles and nothing much happens. The work favors stillness over drama, and is built up slowly, in thin layers, over many sittings.

Based between the studio and wherever the next room of light happens to be, Kiki shows work occasionally and takes on a small number of commissions each year. Get in touch through the contact page.
